# AI SWE Profile Positioning Plan

## Goal

Present Youssef Ahmed as an AI Software Engineer who can:

- design agentic and retrieval-based systems,
- ship the surrounding product and infrastructure end to end,
- move comfortably between AI, full-stack product engineering, mobile, and core CS foundations.

The public profile should read well for both recruiters and technical reviewers.

## Audience

### Recruiters and hiring managers

They need a fast answer to:

- What kind of engineer is this?
- Is he current?
- Has he shipped real systems?
- What makes him different from a generic full-stack candidate?

### Technical interviewers and engineers

They need proof of:

- AI systems depth,
- architecture quality,
- production awareness,
- testing discipline,
- systems fundamentals.

## Positioning Thesis

Primary thesis:

Youssef is an AI Software Engineer specializing in agent systems, RAG, and production-grade product engineering.

Supporting thesis:

- He does not only call model APIs; he builds the full product around them.
- He can work across Python, TypeScript, and Flutter.
- He has strong CS fundamentals through compiler and systems projects.
- His public work shows range across AI, fintech, real-time collaboration, mobile, and backend systems.

## Narrative Pillars

### 1. Applied AI systems

Lead with projects where AI is central to the product:

- Procurement AI
- LegacyLens
- CollabBoard
- Shipyard
- Exquizite

### 2. Production product engineering

Show that the work is not toy AI:

- deployed apps,
- auth, persistence, APIs, dashboards, queues, real-time sync,
- evaluation, telemetry, and safer failure handling,
- frontend and product polish, not just backend experiments.

### 3. Strong engineering fundamentals

Keep one or two classic CS projects visible so the profile does not read as "AI-only":

- PLCProgrammingLanguage
- MemoryManager

### 4. Cross-platform breadth

Show that the profile owner can build across surfaces:

- React / TypeScript web apps,
- Python / FastAPI AI backends,
- Flutter mobile ecosystems,
- systems and language tooling in C++ and Java.

## Recommended README Structure

### 1. Header

Use:

- name,
- concise AI SWE headline,
- two-sentence summary.

The summary should answer:

- what he specializes in,
- what kinds of systems he ships,
- what stacks he works in.

### 2. Current focus

Keep this short and current:

- Procurement AI
- Shipyard
- LegacyLens

This gives the profile a "building now" feel instead of reading like a static archive.

### 3. Selected work

Use a single table with 6 rows max.

Each row should answer:

- what the project is,
- what was technically interesting,
- why a hiring manager should care.

### 4. What I bring

Add a short section that turns the project list into a hiring narrative:

- end-to-end AI product engineering,
- real-time and full-stack system design,
- strong CS fundamentals,
- cross-platform execution.

### 5. Technical toolkit

Group by domain rather than dumping every tool into one sentence:

- AI
- backend
- frontend
- data / infra
- foundations

### 6. Background and contact

Keep this compact:

- B.S. Computer Science, University of Florida '23
- English and Arabic
- LinkedIn and email
